About CivicEvents

A more accessible city starts with all of us.

CivicEvents is a community portal for Rwanda, where residents can find what is happening in their city, stay informed and take part, whatever device or ability they bring.

What the portal offers

Events
Browse upcoming events, filter by date and location, register, cancel and leave feedback after you attend.
Audio announcements
Short spoken updates from the city that you can play, skip through and slow down or speed up.
Captioned promos
Videos about programs and services, with captions and a written transcript.
Notifications
A bell that tells you when a new event, promo or announcement is published.

Two kinds of accounts

Community members

View published content, register for events, give feedback, and manage their own profile and registrations.

Administrators

Publish and manage events, announcements and promos, see who registered, and enable or disable accounts.

Our accessibility commitments

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and skip links.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA color contrast.
  • Images have descriptions, form fields have labels and errors are announced to screen readers.
  • Videos have captions and a transcript; audio can be downloaded as a fallback.
  • Animations are reduced for people who ask their device for less motion.
