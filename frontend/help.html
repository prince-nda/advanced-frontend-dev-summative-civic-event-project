Frequently asked questions

How do I register for an event?+

Open Events, choose an event and press Register. You can register from the list or from the event page. Your registrations are listed under My registrations.

Can I cancel my registration?+

Yes. Open the event or My registrations and choose Cancel registration. Please note that once you cancel, the system does not let you register for the same event again.

How do I leave feedback?+

On an event page, pick a rating from 1 to 5, add an optional comment and submit. You can leave one piece of feedback per event, and you can edit it later.

How do I listen to an announcement?+

Go to Announcements and press the play button. Open the details page for 10-second skip buttons and a speed control.

How do I turn captions on or off?+

Captions are on by default for promo videos. Use the Captions button under the video, or the captions control in the player. The full transcript is shown below the video.

What does the bell mean?+

The bell shows how many notifications you have not read yet. Open it to read them, jump to the related event, promo or announcement, or mark everything as read.

Which keyboard keys can I use?+

Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate, and Esc to close menus, dialogs and the notification panel.

I forgot my password.+

Password reset is not available yet. Please contact the support team at help@civicevents.com and an administrator will help you.

My account was disabled. What now?+

An administrator disabled it. Contact the support team and they can enable it again.
